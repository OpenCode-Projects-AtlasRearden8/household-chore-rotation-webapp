    How this works

    • Rotation wheel: chores step around the wheel week by week, so no one is stuck forever.
    • Strengths: rate each person per chore; the suggestion engine nudges hard jobs toward people who find them easier, without freezing anyone out of the rotation.
    • Trades: swap two chores between two people and log it, including “we already traded yesterday”
    • Skips: log and forgive. Coverage is tracked with love.
    • Review: a weekly kind-numbers page plus a note for next week.
